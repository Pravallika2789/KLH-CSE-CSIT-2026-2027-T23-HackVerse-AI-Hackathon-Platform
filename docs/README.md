## Abstract

HackVerse is an AI-driven adaptive hackathon management and collaboration platform designed to simplify and enhance the complete hackathon lifecycle for organizers, mentors, and participants.

Traditional hackathon platforms mainly provide registration, team management, scheduling, and submission features, but often lack intelligent mechanisms for personalized team formation, collaboration, mentoring, and progress monitoring. HackVerse addresses these limitations by bringing these activities together in a single intelligent platform.

The platform uses artificial intelligence to analyze participant skills, interests, and project preferences and recommend suitable teammates, supporting balanced and compatible team formation. It also provides AI-assisted project ideation, task recommendations, progress insights, and personalized guidance based on the needs of each team.

Organizers can manage participants, challenges, timelines, submissions, evaluations, and event activities through a centralized dashboard, while mentors and judges can interact with teams and provide structured feedback.

The adaptive nature of HackVerse allows the platform to respond to changing team requirements and project progress throughout the hackathon. By combining intelligent recommendations, collaboration features, analytics, and centralized event management, HackVerse aims to create a transparent, scalable, and user-friendly ecosystem that improves teamwork, encourages innovation, and enhances the overall hackathon experience and outcomes.
